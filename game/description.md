A newspaper predicted the last baby. You are that baby. Cross a city made of paper and ink, reclaim F I R S T and rewrite the headline that claims your future is already settled.

Leap across three districts, glide on oversized ears and use a resonant cry to interrupt the printing machines. Change words to change the world: turn STILL into RISING, FIXED into MOVING and CERTAIN into POSSIBLE. Find nine origami stories, listen to the city's residents and correct the final press.

Spend ink on lasting marginalia upgrades, take on optional challenges and uncover alternate ending ranks. Continue into The First Morning to reopen a nursery, restore water, plant a garden and deliver the city's first letter.

A single-player adventure with checkpoints, local saves, keyboard, touch and gamepad controls, painted paper-city scenery and a music-box score.
